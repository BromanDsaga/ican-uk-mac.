Our Leadership Philosophy

Elected Council

Our executive team brings together decades of rigorous industry experience across practice, corporate leadership, public finance, and academia with a forward-thinking approach to institutional stewardship. We believe in transparent leadership, ethical decision-making, and fostering a culture of innovation that empowers every member to excel.

Committees & Advisory Bodies

District Structure

The Executive Committee is supported by specialized standing committees appointed to execute specific portfolios:

Finance Committee

Ensures strict financial control and facilitates sustainable revenue generation.

Media & Communications

Delivers digital, web, social, email, and publication channels for the District.

Membership & Regions

Manages membership records and coordinates chapters in the Midlands, North West & Scotland.

Benevolent Fund & Welfare

Established in 2014 to safeguard member wellbeing, family emergency aid, and benevolence.

Past Chairs & Patrons

Advisory body consisting of all past Chairmen since 1990 providing wisdom and counsel.

Conference Planning

Plans and executes the flagship Annual UK International Accountants' Conference.
