Portal & Membership FAQs

Certified Answers

Password recovery can be performed by completing the following steps:

  1. Click on LOGIN to ICAN-UK >> LOGIN TO My ICAN-UK.
  2. Click on Forgot Password.
  3. Enter the details requested — usually your Year of Induction and click proceed.
  4. Answer your security question to recover your password.

Your login details will be sent automatically to your registered email address. If you don't receive the email from the system for any reason, please contact the General Secretary via generalsecretary@ican-uk.org or via the telephone numbers provided on the contact page (+44 7535 230 605, +44 7894 069 959).

Registration on the ICAN-UK website is strictly meant for ICAN Members ordinarily residing in the United Kingdom.

Registration on the website is subject to approval from the District Secretariat. The login details to the Member's Area will be supplied upon Registration Approval.

If you don't receive an email from the system after approval for any reason, please contact the General Secretary via generalsecretary@ican-uk.org or call +44 7535 230 605 / +44 7894 069 959.

Registered members can update their contact details, employer, address, and email by completing the following steps:

  1. Login to the Member's Area via the 'Login to ICAN-UK' tab on the website or from the homepage navigate to LOGIN to ICAN-UK >> Login to My ICAN-UK.
  2. Click on My Profile.
  3. Review and edit your professional details, contact phone, and practice firm.
  4. Click Save Changes to update the central directory.

As set out in our constitution (About ICAN UK), membership comprises:

  • Fellows of The Institute (FCA): Continuous practice for 5 years or 10 years as an Associate.
  • Associate Members (ACA): Fully qualified through ICAN examinations and formal induction.
  • Affiliate Members: Members of recognized professional accountancy bodies residing in the UK/Ireland.
  • Student Members: Registered students actively pursuing ICAN professional qualifications.

The Technical, CCAB & Skills Committee organizes accredited technical sessions and masterclasses. Attendance at monthly general meetings and the flagship Annual International Accountants' Conference generates verifiable CPD hours recognized by ICAN National Council and CCAB reciprocal bodies.

The Benevolent Fund was founded in 2014 to support members and their dependent families in times of severe illness, bereavement, acute hardship, or relocation distress. All applications are treated under strict confidentiality by the Welfare Board Trustees. See Benevolent Fund Details & Form.
