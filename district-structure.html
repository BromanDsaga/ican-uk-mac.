Institutional Architecture

The District Governance Structure

ICAN Statutory Body

The governance structure of the ICAN UK & District Society comprises the Executive Committee supported by specialized Standing Committees and the Committee of Past Chairs and Patrons. The Executive Committee is constitutionally responsible for co-ordinating and controlling the affairs of the Society, regulating membership, and catering for the professional and social interests of members across the United Kingdom.

Charter Foundation

Statutory Recognition

Chartered Accountants Act 1965 (Act No. 15). The UK District Society operates under the direct charter and delegated authority of the ICAN Governing Council headquartered in Victoria Island, Lagos.

Global Presence

The Foremost International District

One of four prestigious international district societies located outside Nigeria (United Kingdom, USA, Canada, and Malaysia), serving as the principal bridge between ICAN and UK financial institutions.

Membership Strength

550+ Chartered Members

Connecting Fellows (FCA), Associates (ACA), Affiliates, and registered students across major UK commercial centres, multinational corporations, public bodies, and practice firms.

Operational Flow

Four-Tier Governance Hierarchy

Accountability
Tier 1 • Supreme Authority

The Annual General Meeting (AGM)

The sovereign assembly of all members in good standing. The AGM receives the audited annual accounts, reviews the Chairman’s annual report, ratifies major policy decisions, and elects executive officers in accordance with the District Bye-Laws.

Tier 2 • Executive Authority

The Executive Committee

Comprises 10 elected officers and the Immediate Past Chair (IPC) in an ex-officio capacity: Chairman, Vice Chairman, Deputy Vice Chairman, General Secretary, Assistant General Secretary, Financial Secretary, Treasurer, Technical Secretary, Social Secretary, Membership Secretary, and Ex-Officio members. Responsible for the overall direction, financial integrity, and representation of the District.

Tier 3 • Operational Implementation

Standing Committees

Appointed operational sub-committees constituted by the Executive Committee to drive specific programmes: Finance, Media & Communications, Membership & Regions, Socials & Events, International Conference, Technical & CCAB Liaison, Ethics, Benevolent Fund, and Partnerships & Sponsors. Open to participation by all members.

Tier 4 • Advisory & Institutional Memory

Committee of Past Chairs and Patrons

Chaired by seasoned past leaders (currently Chaired by Mr. David Fadipe, FCA with Mr. Segun Omorayewa, FCA as Secretary). Advises the Executive Committee on constitutional matters, long-term strategic direction, and external institutional relationships.

UK Geographical Coverage

Regional Footprint & Zonal Coordination

Nationwide Reach

Under the remit of the Membership and Regions Committee (chaired by Mr. Tope Imevbore with Ms. Chinasa Mbachu as Secretary), ICAN UK actively engages chartered accountants across all regions of the United Kingdom, ensuring local access to CPD, networking, and welfare support.

Central Headquarters

London & South East Chapter

Central operational base and home to the official Secretariat (Coker Isah & Coker, 74 Church Road, Crystal Palace, London SE19 2EZ). Hosts the primary monthly technical assemblies and annual conferences.

Midlands Chapter

Birmingham & Central England

Covers members practicing across the West Midlands, Warwickshire, Leicestershire, and Nottinghamshire. Organizes regional networking seminars and professional development meetups.

Northern Chapter

Manchester, Leeds & North West

Dynamic hub for members in Greater Manchester, Merseyside, Lancashire, and Yorkshire. Facilitates regional study groups and links with regional accountancy firms.

Northern Territories

Scotland & Northern Ireland

Reaches chartered accountants residing in Edinburgh, Glasgow, Aberdeen, and Belfast, providing digital hybrid access to meetings, voting, and benevolence support.
