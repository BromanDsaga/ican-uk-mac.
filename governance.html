Legal Foundation

Statutory Mandate & Council Regulations

Act No. 15 of 1965

The Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria was established by the Chartered Accountants Act No. 15 of 1965. Under the provisions of this Act, the Governing Council is empowered to charter District Societies both within Nigeria and across key international jurisdictions to maintain the roll of members and ensure rigorous adherence to international standards.

Professional Standards

Code of Ethics & Disciplinary Protocols

IFAC Aligned

Under the oversight of the Ethics Committee (chaired by Yetunde Raimi-Odimayo with Abiola Elizabeth James as Secretary), every ICAN member in the UK adheres to the fundamental ethical tenets of the International Federation of Accountants (IFAC):

⚖️
Integrity

Straightforward and honest in all professional relationships.

🎯
Objectivity

No bias, conflict of interest, or undue influence of others.

📚
Professional Competence

Continuous attainment of professional knowledge and skill.

🔒
Confidentiality

Respecting the confidentiality of information acquired in practice.
