Institutional Mandate

Our Vision & Mission

Official Mandate

🎯 Our Vision

"ICAN UK District's vision is to support the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria in fulfilling its mission 'To Produce World-Class Chartered Accountants, Regulate and Continuously Enhance Their Ethical Standards and Technical Competence in the Public Interest'. Our vision is therefore to contribute towards the fulfilment of ICAN's mission by presenting ICAN as a professional body of world-class accountants with high ethical standards."

⚡ Our Mission

"Our mission is to help our members achieve professional excellence and social well-being as well as project a positive image of the Nigerian accountant."

Through sustained professional development, rigorous ethical oversight, and a strong collective community in the UK, we serve as the vital international conduit for Nigerian accounting excellence abroad.

Strategy & Roadmap

Strategic Objectives

5 Key Pillars

We have developed five key strategic objectives which lead to the fulfilment of our mission and vision across the United Kingdom:

01
Effective Representation of ICAN in the United Kingdom
02
Improving the Professional Competence of our members
03
Promoting Cohesion among our members and the Nigerian community
04
Promoting our members' social well-being and welfare
05
Ensuring the Financial Viability and sustainability of the District

Strategy Implementation & Review

1
Communications & Digital Dissemination

Improve the quality of our communications using our website and publications to foster participation of our members; disseminate key technical information that will improve our members' competencies; provide better information; promote positive values and serve as an image of ICAN to the outside world.

2
Charity & Community Empowerment

Focus our charity work on promoting a positive image of the Nigerian accountant. We will support charities in the areas of empowerment and capacity building for people who can project our values and image.

3
Mentorship & Professional Growth Schemes

We will strengthen our mentoring scheme to serve the needs of our members and possibly the wider community and work with stakeholders to establish volunteer or work-experience schemes that can benefit both parties.

Community & Admissions

Who We Are & Membership Categories

550+ Members

ICAN UK & District Society is one of four District Societies of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria (ICAN) located outside Nigeria. Established in 1988, the District is "The Foremost International District" and has grown to a current membership of 550+ finance professionals across England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland.

Official Membership Categories

Fellowship

Fellows of The Institute (FCA)

Associate members of the Institute who have been in continuous practice on their own account for five years, or those who are not in practice as an Associate of the Institute for a period of ten years, are eligible to apply for transfer to Fellowship of the Institute.

Designation: FCA
Chartered

Associate Members (ACA)

Members who have passed the rigorous professional examinations of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria or have fulfilled a prescribed requirement as laid down by the Institute and have been formally admitted to membership.

Designation: ACA
Reciprocal

Affiliate Members

Members of any professional accountancy body recognised by ICAN who are ordinarily resident in the United Kingdom or Ireland. Affiliate Membership remains valid from admission to the conclusion of either the next 2 consecutive induction programmes held by ICAN in Nigeria or the next UK District Society induction.

UK & Ireland Resident
Future Talent

Student Members

Students registered with any professional accountancy body recognised by ICAN who are ordinarily resident in the United Kingdom, actively pursuing certification and qualification.
