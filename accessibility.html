ACCESSIBILITY STATEMENT

Our commitment to ensuring the ICAN UK & District Society digital platform is fully accessible, inclusive, and compliant with Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA.

Deliverable #20 & #29 • Universal Access

Conformance Status & Standards

WCAG 2.2 Level AA

The Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria (UK & District Society) is dedicated to providing an accessible experience for all visitors, including persons with visual, auditory, cognitive, or motor impairments. We strive to meet and exceed Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA conformance.

1. Perceivable

All non-text content possesses descriptive alternative text (alt attributes). High color contrast ratios (minimum 4.5:1 for normal text) ensure readable typography across desktop and mobile.

2. Operable

All website functionality, form fields, and navigation elements are fully operable via standard keyboard interface without keyboard traps. Visible focus rings guide keyboard navigators.

3. Understandable

Clear headings, intuitive semantic HTML landmarks (<header>, <nav>, <main>, <footer>), and descriptive error notifications ensure straightforward interaction.

4. Robust

Built on modern W3C standards with ARIA roles to ensure compatibility with screen readers (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver), speech recognition tools, and browser zoom engines up to 200%.
