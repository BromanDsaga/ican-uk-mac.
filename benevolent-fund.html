THE BENEVOLENT FUND

Established in 2014 by District pioneers to serve as a practical demonstration of our mission to help members achieve professional excellence and social well-being through confidential compassion and emergency financial assistance.

Mandate & Heritage

Est. 2014
"This fund was set up by the pioneers of ICAN UK and the District Society in 2014. It is to serve as a practical way of demonstrating the District’s commitment to part of its mission to ‘…help our members achieve professional excellence and social well-being…’"

The ICAN UK Benevolent Fund operates as an independent, compassionate safety net governed by strict confidentiality guidelines. It exists to assist chartered accountants, associate members, registered students, and their immediate families when unforeseen life events, prolonged illness, family bereavement, or acute financial hardship arise.

🔒 100% Confidential

Every request is reviewed under strict anonymity protocols by the appointed Welfare Trustees. Names are never disclosed to the wider membership.

⚡ Rapid Assessment

Urgent applications for emergency medical or repatriation distress are reviewed within 48 to 72 hours by the Welfare Board.

🤝 Comprehensive Aid

Assistance combines one-off emergency grants, temporary subsistence, integration assistance for newcomers, and career return mentoring.

Criteria & Guidelines

Eligibility & Assistance Categories

Fair Assessment

The Fund provides support across several defined categories of hardship for members ordinarily resident in the United Kingdom:

Medical & Critical Illness Emergency

Support for members facing unexpected medical costs, rehabilitation expenses, or loss of income due to severe illness.

Bereavement & Dependent Support

Compassionate assistance granted to the surviving spouse, children, or immediate dependents of deceased members.

Career Distress & Sudden Job Loss

Interim emergency relief and professional resume/interview coaching for members affected by sudden redundancy or displacement.

Relocation & UK Integration Aid

Targeted assistance for newly arrived Nigerian chartered accountants navigating initial integration hurdles in the UK.
