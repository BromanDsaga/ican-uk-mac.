The Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria — UK & District Society is a distinguished professional community. We represent, support and elevate Nigerian chartered accountants living and working in the United Kingdom.

Our work spans professional development, ethical standards, welfare, representation and cultural connection — grounded in the rigorous heritage of ICAN and shaped for a modern global economy. We are both institutional and human, premium and approachable.

Aims & Objectives

FROM PEOPLE TO PURPOSE — FOUR PILLARS THAT HOLD US.

STANDARDS
Upholding ICAN ethical codes, technical excellence and continuous professional development for members in the UK.
WELFARE
Benevolence, integration support, career guidance and a safety net for members and families.
REPRESENTATION
A collective voice for Nigerian chartered accountants in UK policy, finance and professional bodies.
PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT
CPD, masterclasses, mentorship and pathways for students to fellowship.
