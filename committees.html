OUR COMMITTEES & COMMITTEE LEADS

The Executive Committee constitutes specialized standing committees to assist in implementing its statutory programmes. Officers are appointed by the Executive Committee, with membership open to all members of the District Society.

Member Participation

Standing Committee Appointments & Membership

Open to All Members

"The Executive Committee constitutes various standing committees to assist in implementing its programmes. Officers of the standing committees are appointed by the Executive Committee while membership is open to all members of the District Society. There is also a Committee of Past Chairs and Patrons, where membership comprises all Past Chairs and Patrons of the District Society."

Volunteer for a Committee → Contribute your skills to District initiatives and earn CPD credits.

International Conference Committee

Flagship

The Committee's objective is to organize, curate, and deliver the District's flagship annual International Accountants' Conference, overseeing keynote speakers, technical themes, venue logistics, CPD accreditation, and the Gala Dinner.

Committee Chair Tayo Animashaun, FCA
Secretary Kemi Phillips

Technical, CCAB & Skills Committee

CPD

The Committee's objective is to organize technical masterclasses and workshops accredited by The Institute to fulfill the technical skills and professional development needs of members, and foster harmonious relations with UK CCAB bodies (ICAEW, ACCA, ICAS, CIPFA).

Committee Chair Seyi Bamisaye, FCA
Secretary Franklin Nakpodia

Finance Committee

Stewardship

The Committee's objective is to ensure strict financial control, budgetary oversight, and transparency in all District affairs, as well as facilitate sustainable revenue generation and financial planning.

Committee Chair Benjamin Ajayi
Secretary Nnewima Nwafor-Orizu

Media & Communications Committee

Digital & Press

The Committee's objective is to deliver all media and communication needs of the District — across Print, Email, Web, and Social Media — as well as deliver all Information Technology infrastructure for the District Society.

Committee Chair Nnenna Anyanwu
Secretary Nojeem Yusuf

Socials & Events Committee

Community

The Committee's objective is to facilitate members' social wellbeing, foster professional fellowship, and organize all District social gatherings, including the Annual Dinner & Dance, networking mixers, and cultural evenings.

Committee Chair Wunmi Hassan, FCA
Secretary Olayinka Oluwi

Ethics Committee

Integrity

The Committee's objectives are to develop a code of conduct for members of ICAN UK & District Society, ensure meeting protocols and timeliness, and deliver appropriate Bye-Law concessions to safeguard institutional harmony.

Committee Chair Yetunde Raimi-Odimayo
Secretary Abiola Elizabeth James

Membership & Regions Committee

Admissions

The Committee's objectives are to manage and safeguard the membership database, reach out to dormant members, expand the District's base, provide structured mentorship, and actively involve members in regional chapters across the UK.

Committee Chair Tope Imevbore
Secretary Chinasa Mbachu

Benevolent Fund Committee

Safety Net

Established by District pioneers in 2014 as a tangible demonstration of our commitment to member welfare. Operates under confidential criteria to provide emergency financial aid and compassionate assistance to members and families in need.

Governance Appointed Welfare Board
Established 2014 (Charter Mandate)

Partnership & Sponsors Committee

Corporate

Proactively engages existing and prospective corporate sponsors in both the UK and Nigeria, negotiating multi-year partnerships, commercial exhibitor packages, and alliances with complementary professional bodies.

Portfolio Focus Corporate Partnerships
Geography United Kingdom & Nigeria

Committee of Past Chairs & Patrons

Elders Council

Comprises all Past Chairs and Patrons of the District Society since 1990. Advises the Executive Committee on constitutional questions referred to it and supports major institutional assemblies and national council relations.

Committee Chair David Fadipe, FCA
Secretary Segun Omorayewa, FCA
